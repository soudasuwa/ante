ante guestbook

Anyone can sign — no account needed. Each post carries a little computing time as proof, and entries are grouped by how much their author spent.

connecting…

Your Ante key is an identity your own Freenet node holds for you — no signup, no email, nothing to remember. Posting spends a little computing time with it, as proof you are not a spam script. Every level is a normal post; spending more places your message higher up the page. Your node will ask before anything is spent.

working…

Your name and message are locked while this runs: the proof is tied to their exact wording, so changing a character would throw the work away.

Posted as — — your Ante key, held by your own Freenet node and shared with every app that uses ante, so work you put in here counts elsewhere too.
