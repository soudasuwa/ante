Each extra bit roughly doubles the work (in your browser: ~18 a second or two, ~20 around ten seconds, ~24 a minute or more). The grind often clears a bit or two past your target — that's your real level. The registry only ever raises it, so aim above where you are.

Back up this identity

Your identity is a key held by the ante delegate on your node. If that node's storage is lost, it's gone — unless you saved this code. You can restore it here, on this node or another.

Anyone with this code controls your identity. Store it somewhere safe, like a password manager — never paste it into a site you don't trust.
